About Research Teaching CV EN / ES

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the University of Gothenburg and an affiliate of the LSE Hub for Equal Representation. In 2025/26, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at the Stone Centre at UCL. I completed my PhD in economics at the London School of Economics in 2025.

I do research at the intersection of family, gender and development economics. My work studies how family formation (partnership, fertility and motherhood) shapes women's economic lives and their exposure to violence. I combine quasi-experimental methods and simple economic models to understand the constraints women face within households, and which policies can relax them.
